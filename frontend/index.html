How this feature will work

✓ Camera starts only after your action and permission.

✓ Face landmarks are processed locally in this browser.
Analysis status: Face landmarks only
MediaPipe Face Landmarker detects facial landmarks and draws a face-mesh overlay locally in this browser. Webcam frames are not uploaded to NeuroSense or stored by this feature. The MediaPipe library and model load from Google's CDN, and its service may collect usage metrics. This feature does not infer emotion or stress.
